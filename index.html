About The Doctor

Meet Dr. Piyush Pahadiya
Your Skin & Hair Expert

Dr. Piyush Pahadiya is a highly qualified Dermatologist, Cosmetologist & Hair Specialist with over 9 years of clinical excellence. He completed his MBBS and MD (Skin) from the prestigious AIIMS New Delhi and is a proud member of IADVL (Indian Association of Dermatologists, Venereologists and Leprologists).

He has served as Assistant Professor at JLN Medical College, Ajmer and PIMS, Udaipur. His research papers have been published in various international and national journals. Dr. Pahadiya believes in treating every patient like family — listening carefully, diagnosing accurately, and delivering personalized treatments based on your unique skin tone and concerns.
